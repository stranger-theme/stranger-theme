# Stranger Things Theme - Color Palette Research

## General Stranger Things Aesthetic
The Stranger Things aesthetic is characterized by moody yet vibrant colors that evoke the 1980s retro feel. The primary color palette includes:
- Maroon red
- Navy blue
- Dark teal
- Mustard yellow
- Burnt orange

These colors are often less saturated and flatter than modern colors to create an authentic vintage feel. Most retro color palettes exclude bright colors to imitate the printing capabilities of the 1980s.

## Theme Variant: Upside Down
The Upside Down has a distinctive dark and eerie palette:
- Blues (various shades, particularly darker blues)
- Blacks
- Desaturated tones
- Contrasting with occasional glowing elements

## Theme Variant: Vecna
Based on the character Vecna from Season 4:
- Dominated by blue tones
- Dark, textured appearance
- Organic, almost fungal-like textures
- Cold, unsettling color temperature

## Theme Variant: Starcourt Mall
The Starcourt Mall represents the vibrant 80s commercial aesthetic:
- Neon pinks and purples
- Bright blues
- Cream/ivory accents
- Typical 80s mall aesthetics with bright signage

## Theme Variant: Hawkins
Representing the small-town America aesthetic:
- Warm, muted tones
- Autumnal colors (especially in early seasons)
- Suburban color palette with browns and greens
- Nostalgic, slightly desaturated look

## Theme Variant: The Lab
Hawkins Lab has a clinical, institutional aesthetic:
- Sterile whites
- Institutional greens
- Cold blues
- Harsh fluorescent lighting effects
- Clinical, government facility appearance

## Theme Variant: Tigers (Hawkins School)
Based on the Hawkins school mascot:
- School colors (likely orange/black for Tigers)
- Vintage school aesthetic
- Muted academic tones
- Nostalgic 80s school environment colors

## Theme Variant: Demogorgon
The monster's distinctive look:
- Fleshy pinks and reds
- Dark, organic textures
- Contrasting with the blue tones of the Upside Down
- Biological, almost fungal-like appearance

## Lighting Considerations
Lighting plays a crucial role in the Stranger Things aesthetic:
- String lights (especially Christmas lights)
- LED and fairy lights
- Artificial lighting is preferred over natural lighting
- Colored lighting (especially in supernatural scenes)

## Visual Style Notes
- The show employs a grainy retro visual style
- Combines elements of indie aesthetic and trippy/psychedelic influences
- Strong contrast between normal world and supernatural elements
- Heavy use of shadows and dramatic lighting
